Frequently asked questions

Common fleet and program questions to help you move faster.

Will PillowFlow fit our existing fleet vehicles?
PillowFlow is designed to fit most car and truck seats with a low-profile shape and adjustable strap. We recommend validating fit in a sample vehicle before fleet rollout.
Is this a medical device?
No. PillowFlow is a comfort aid intended to reduce thigh pressure and improve seated comfort while driving. It does not diagnose, treat, or prevent disease.
How do fleets place orders and manage setup?
Fleet orders are handled through a dedicated quote process. We can help align onboarding documentation, cleaning guidance, and support steps.
What should drivers expect during the pilot?
Drivers should expect a simple fit and adjustment process, then daily use during routes with feedback collected through the pilot program.
